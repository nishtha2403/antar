02

The two meters

The constraint is that these must never combine into a score. Two identical bars side by side invite exactly that — the eye subtracts one from the other. So they are not the same object. Quantity is a filled bar. Time is a dated ruler with a tick per year and a marker at today. They share no geometry, no fill and no axis, and there is no arithmetic that visually relates them.
