अंतर ANTAR

About

अंतर the distance between two things

Antar records what the government said it would achieve, measures what has happened using the government's own published data, and shows the distance between the two.

It is not a scorecard, a ranking, or an assessment of whether any government is doing well. Where context is needed, it is attributed to whoever said it.

Who runs it

Nishtha Sharma. One person, currently the author, the verifier and the only reviewer. That is a real weakness and it is written down rather than hidden.
